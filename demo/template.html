What Woody would send you

The weekly review is the Analyst's message. The conclusion is the Concluder's draft, and it stays marked provisional until the charter's final-call date.

Who does what

You approve the charter, drop in a spreadsheet or a voice note, and make the final call. The roles below do the rest.

Framer

Turns an idea into hypotheses, pass lines and decision rules, and refuses a charter with gaps.

woody_init_experiment

Collector

Takes bookings, surveys, notes and photos with consent, and links each to the session and family code.

woody_add_records, woody_import_csv, woody_log_note, woody_register_photo

Analyst

Computes every number exactly in code and flags thin or missing evidence.

woody_review

Concluder

Writes go, pivot or stop. Every claim lists the records behind it.

woody_conclude

Memory keeper

Answers “why” from connected context in HydraDB, not from guesses.

woody_ask
